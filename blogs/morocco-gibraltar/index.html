Morocco & Gibraltar

DAY I

We arrived in Marrakesh Medina Airport at about 7:06 a.m. Since check-in at our riad was from 3:00 p.m., we decided to walk from the airport all the way to the medina on foot. The road was long, straight, and full of traffic, with not much to see along the way.

Upon arriving in the medina, we bought khobz, the traditional Arab bread, for breakfast along with traditional tea. After that, we walked around the medina until we were allowed to check in to our accommodation. Just after arriving, we fell asleep because we had not slept at Malaga Airport the previous night.

After waking up, we went to get some tagine and then went straight back to bed.

DAY II

The following day, we visited Ben Youssef Madrasa, which was at the time the largest Islamic college in the Maghreb. After that, we spent the rest of the day exploring the medina and had couscous for dinner.

DAY III

On the third day, we didn't have much to do. So we took a stroll around the newer part of the town.

We went to the train station, to which we would be returning in two days time. On the way there, we saw the Théâtre Royal - one of the few interesting buildings on the way.

After having a small lunch, we went to visit Jardin Majorelle. A beautiful garden offering some shade in the heat of the city. One ticket cost us 95 DH for the garden only, after presenting a valid student ID.

DAY IV

The next day, we decided that we had had enough of Marrakesh and wanted to travel farther afield.

We went to a bus station near Jardin Sidi Mimoun, unsure of what to expect. To our surprise, a modern air-conditioned bus awaited us. We boarded the L25 and headed towards Ourika. A single ticket cost us around 7.5 DH.

After that, we wanted to head up into the mountains, so we found ourselves a taxi. Unfortunately, we couldn't find a shared taxi because of my poor French, so we ended up paying 200 DH for a taxi all the way to Sti Fadma.

Upon our arrival, a tour guide immediately approached us to offer his services. In the end, he was a friendly guy, so we decided to take his offer. The short journey was beautiful and the nature was amazing, so I'll let the photos speak for themselves.

On the way back, we managed to get a shared taxi all the way to Marrakech, thanks to our tour guide. It cost us 120 DH.

DAY V

On the fifth day, we took a train to Rabat, the capital city of Morocco.

The train was a classic coupe train, and the journey was quite pleasant. On the way, some interesting scenery was visible.

After arriving in Rabat, we went to our lodging, right on the outskirts of the medina. After a bit of a break, we explored the rest of the city. Walking around the medina, going to the beach and Bab Oudaya, which had a beautiful large graveyard nearby.

Later in the evening we wanted to visit the Royal Palace. Unfortunately, the only way to visit it is with a guided tour.

DAY VI

The next day, we took a train to Tangier. This train was a modern carriage of the same type as the French TGV. A high-speed track is laid between Kenitra and Tangier, with plans to extend the network all the way to Marrakesh.

Upon arriving, we had some time to spare, so we explored the beach, which is about 10 minutes on foot from the train station. Unfortunately, when we arrived at our booked flat, the address on Booking didn't exist, and a bunch of guys were waiting near the apartment complex. They offered us an apartment for the night at a higher price. I made the mistake of accepting their offer, and we ended up staying in a dirty and smelly apartment.

What didn't help was Morocco winning a match in the World Cup, so we got very little sleep that night.

DAY VII

On the last proper day, we took a ferry to Tarifa. We had to wake up a bit early, but otherwise the journey was smooth.

Then we took a bus to La Línea de la Concepción, where we had our next lodging. After putting our stuff down, I took a quick stroll to Gibraltar. I ran up the rock and, although there is normally an entrance fee, if you arrive late (or early) enough there is no one manning the entrance booth, so you can enter for free. The view up there was stunning, although the weather was a bit too hot for my liking. Even the monkeys were not very present.

DAY VIII

On the last day, we took a bus to Malaga Airport, where our journey officially ended.
